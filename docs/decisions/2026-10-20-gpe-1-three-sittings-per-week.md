# GPE-1 amended: at most three sittings per week (GP-E unchanged at 4 hours/week)

- **Date:** 2026-10-20
- **Actor:** Aaron (the architect)
- **Concerns:** GPE-1, the operator's ruling of 2026-09-30 that operator acts are batched into at most two sittings per
  week; Plan v1.1 s12's GP-E ("4 h/week, two sittings <= 2 h")

## Why this record exists

The operator proposed raising the sitting count by one. Three of the four weeks to date held more than two sittings
(week 1 overrun accepted; week 3 three sittings, recorded by Ops) while operator hours stayed well inside GP-E (week 3:
1.83 h of 4.0), so the count bound, not the hours, was the constraint in practice. Dev recommended keeping the hour
envelope fixed so a third sitting adds no load.

## The ruling, verbatim

> Ruling 2026-10-20 (Aaron): GPE-1 is amended to at most three sittings per week, effective week 4 (from 2026-10-20). GP-E stays 4 hours per week, sittings of no more than 2 hours each.

## Effect

- From week 4 (2026-10-20): at most **three** sittings per week.
- GP-E is unchanged: **4 hours per week**, each sitting **no more than 2 hours**.
- Earlier weeks are not re-counted; week 3's three sittings stand as recorded against the two-sitting rule then in force.
- Dev batches operator acts under the new count (the sitting count is Dev's to manage, per GPE-1).
